Frequently Asked Questions

Why is direct freelance so much cheaper than agencies?

Agencies charge ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,50,000+ because they maintain corporate offices, sales executives, and middle managers. By working directly with Dev Sharma, you eliminate all middleman markups and get 1-on-1 developer attention at a fraction of the cost.

Do you handle CBSE / ICSE Mandatory Disclosures?

Yes, 100%. I create a dedicated public disclosure section adhering strictly to the board's official format (Appendix IX) with downloadable PDF links for affiliation letters, safety certificates, fee structure, and staff details.

How fast can our school website go live?

Typically 5 to 10 working days once your school details and photos are shared. Urgent requirements can also be prioritized.

Can school administrative staff upload circulars easily?

Yes! I provide simple handover training so your office staff can upload PDF circulars and event photos in seconds, or you can send them directly to me for instant upload.

Do we get complete ownership of domain and hosting?

Yes, 100% full ownership. Unlike agencies that lock you in proprietary hosting, your school retains complete master credentials, source code, and domain control.
